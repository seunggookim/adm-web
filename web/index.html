Musical surprise and uncertainty from audio

Information content (IC, surprise) and entropy (uncertainty) of each 93-ms frame, from the audio-based autoregressive diffusion model (ADM; Bjare et al., 2026). Everything is computed in this browser: your audio files are never uploaded.

CSV columns as admcompute.py: Frame, Time (s, start of the frame), IC_<t> and Entropy_<t> (nats) for each noise level t; leading and trailing silence (60 dB) is NaN. Folders are searched recursively; the ZIP and the output folder keep their structure and add adm-web-log.json (settings, model version, frames and time per file). Up to about 7 min per file. Entropy takes most of the time (32 Monte Carlo samples, with a variance reduction: frame-wise values repeat with r ≈ .97–.99 at t = 0.6); for entropy, noise levels must be multiples of 0.01.

Privacy. Your audio is decoded and analysed on your own computer and is never uploaded. The page downloads only the ONNX Runtime library (jsDelivr) and the model files (this site).

Model. Perceptually aligned autoregressive rectified flow (pa-audioic; Bjare, Cantisani, Pasini, Lattner & Widmer, 2026, EUSIPCO) on Music2Latent latents (pa-music2latent; Pasini, Lattner & Fazekas, 2024, ISMIR), from the Institute of Computational Perception, Johannes Kepler University Linz (github.com/CPJKU/pa-audioic). The weights are licensed under CC BY-NC 4.0; for this page they were converted to ONNX without changing them. Non-commercial use only.

Differences from pa-audioic. The encoder, which turns the audio into 93-ms latent frames, runs on 30-s windows inside pa-audioic's 60-s chunks (a whole 60-s chunk does not fit in browser memory), which changes the latents slightly; the model's predictive context still spans the whole excerpt (up to about 7 min). Like pa-audioic, IC and entropy are Monte Carlo estimates: this page's values differ from a pa-audioic run about as much as two pa-audioic runs with different random seeds.

Length. The model's context holds at most 4,600 frames (about 7.1 min), so longer files are not analysed. IC depends strongly on how much music precedes a frame, so cutting a longer piece into separate excerpts gives each excerpt its own start, with higher IC at the beginning.
